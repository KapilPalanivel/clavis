Refund Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

We want you to be happy with Clavis Pro. If it's not right for you, we offer a 14-day money-back guarantee.

How it works

  • Within 14 days of your purchase, email clavis.enc@gmail.com from the address you bought with, and we'll refund you in full.
  • You don't need a detailed reason — a quick note of what didn't work helps us improve, but it isn't required.
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method by our payment provider. It can take a few business days for the money to appear.

Try before you buy

Because Clavis Free lets you use the app and its full-strength encryption before upgrading, we encourage you to try it first so Pro is an easy decision.

After a refund

Once refunded, your Pro license is deactivated and the Pro features return to the Free set. Your encrypted files and your Free features keep working normally.

Contact

Need a refund or have a question? Email clavis.enc@gmail.com.
