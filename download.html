Getting started

Three minutes, start to locked.

  1. Install

    Windows: double-click Clavis-Setup.exe — it installs just for you, no administrator prompt. Linux: sudo apt install ./Clavis-linux.deb

  2. Create your vault

    Open Clavis from the Start menu or your apps menu and choose a strong password. Clavis coaches you while you type.

  3. Save your recovery key

    Write it down or print it. It's your way back in if you ever forget the password. More about it →

Questions

Where are my files and vaults kept?

On your PC only. Encrypted files stay where you put them, and your vault settings live in your Windows user folder.

Can I encrypt straight from File Explorer?

Yes. Right-click a file or folder → Clavis → Encrypt, Decrypt or Verify; folders also offer Add to Watch Folder. On Windows 11, choose "Show more options" first. You can turn the menu off in Settings → Explorer.

Can I open an encrypted file without decrypting it?

Yes, with Clavis Pro: double-click the .enc file and it opens in its usual app without leaving a decrypted copy on your PC. Save your edits back into the encrypted file, or re-lock it when you are done. With Clavis Free, double-clicking offers to decrypt the file.

How do updates work?

Clavis checks once a day (or when you choose Settings → Updates → Check now). Updates are small, and you choose when the app restarts.

What if I forget my password?

Use the recovery key Clavis gave you when you created your vault — choose “Forgot password?” on the unlock screen.

Can I uninstall it?

Yes — from Windows Settings → Apps. Your encrypted files and vaults are left untouched, so keep Clavis (or reinstall it) to open them.

Does Clavis upload my files?

No. Everything happens on your computer. Clavis only goes online to check for updates.
