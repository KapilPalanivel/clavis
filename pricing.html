Why one-time, not a subscription?

Most encryption tools bill you every year. Clavis Pro and Premium are single payments — you buy it once and it's yours, including future updates. It's an app that runs on your PC, not a service you rent.

  • Pay once, keep it. No renewals, no expiry date.
  • Every future update included. New features arrive inside the app.
  • Works offline, no account. Your licence is checked once, then never again.
  • Grow later. Move from Pro to Premium by paying only the difference.

Questions

What's actually free?

Encrypting and decrypting individual files with full-strength AES-256-GCM, one vault, the recovery key, integrity checks and secure delete. The security is identical to Pro — Pro adds power features, never stronger crypto.

What's the difference between Pro and Premium?

Pro is for working with lots of files on your PC: folders, several vaults, watch folders, key-file 2FA, secure notes, opening files without a decrypted copy and browsing encrypted folders. Premium adds everything that reaches beyond one PC: encrypting straight into your cloud folder, the command line for scripts and scheduled backups, sharing encrypted files with other people, one-file vault backups and previous versions of files you edit.

Can I upgrade from Pro to Premium later?

Yes, and you only pay the difference. Email clavis.enc@gmail.com from the address you bought Pro with and we'll send you an upgrade code.

When can I buy Pro or Premium?

Very soon — the store is being set up. Email clavis.enc@gmail.com and you'll hear the day it opens. Meanwhile, Clavis Free has the full encryption.

How do I activate Pro or Premium?

Your license key will arrive by email right after you buy. Open Clavis → Settings → License → paste the key → Activate. The key is checked once online; after that Pro works offline, with no account.

One licence, how many PCs?

Your license covers your own computers. To move it to a new PC, open Settings → License → Remove license on the old one first, then activate on the new one.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes — that's what the Free version is for. It gives you the full app and full-strength encryption, so you can be sure before upgrading. Because you can try everything for free, Pro purchases are final.

Do I have to pay for updates?

No. Pro and Premium are one-time and include all future updates.
