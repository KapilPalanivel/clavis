Questions

What's actually free?

Encrypting and decrypting individual files with full-strength AES-256-GCM, one vault, the recovery key, integrity checks and secure delete. The security is identical to Pro — Pro adds power features, never stronger crypto.

How do I activate Pro?

Your license key arrives by email right after you buy. Open Clavis → Settings → Clavis Pro → paste the key → Activate. The key is checked once online; after that Pro works offline, with no account.

One licence, how many PCs?

Your license covers your own computers. To move it to a new PC, open Settings → Clavis Pro → Remove license on the old one first, then activate on the new one.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes — that's what the Free version is for. It gives you the full app and full-strength encryption, so you can be sure before upgrading. Because you can try everything for free, Pro purchases are final.

Do I have to pay for updates?

No. Pro is one-time and includes all future updates.
